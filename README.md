A short poem about permission — the power to ask and to be given — written for the make.fun contest "The poem about permission".

> I stopped waiting
> for the knock to come from outside.
> I knocked, softly,
> on my own door —
>
> and opened it.
> Nobody gave me the power.
> I remembered it was mine.

`main.py` writes the poem (157 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
